Rules
Style guide v3.1.5 quick check
  • New segment only after ≥1s silence; shorter gaps stay in one segment.
  • Every segment ends with punctuation (। ? ! …); tags go after it.
  • One-word stutter আমি - আমি; multi-word restart or cut-off word -- + space; resume with ...শব্দ.
  • Numbers: ১৪ {PRO: চোদ্দ}. Idioms (একমাত্র, প্রথমত) stay words.
  • Wrong word that is a real word: keep as said. Non-word, clear intent: সঠিক {MIS: যা বলা}. Unclear: phonetic. Unintelligible: [অবোধগম্য].
  • English proper nouns in English (iPhone, YouTube). Acronyms as-is.
  • Fillers only: আহ আহা অউ এহ আ অ হুম হুহ হু-হু ওহ ওহহো উহ.
  • *জোর* rare. Emotion tags under 1% of words, under 50 words each.
  • Don't tag ordinary breaths or lip sounds. Never write channel bleed.
  • Timestamps within 50ms: no clipping, no padding. Hard parts: use Both and listen 5–10s around.
